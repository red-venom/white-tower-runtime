# Forty-weave campaign catalogue

This is a complete numerical content proposal. It does not start play or verify a live engine. Read it with [Mechanics](mechanics.md) and [Curriculum](curriculum.md).

Every entry is **version 1, campaign invention, with proposed house-rule availability in 950 NE**. No entry claims a verified canonical name, Flow recipe or numerical effect. “Healing” and “Shield” use familiar setting terms, but their rules here remain inventions. No primary source has been verified for an individual recipe. Record the source as this document's stable weave ID, not an invented book citation.

This source classification is explicit for **W01–W40**. A lore review can later add a verified source and change the classification. A mechanical change needs a new version. None of these entries grants Traveling, Skimming, balefire, resurrection, mind control, Dreamwalking, severing or a rare Talent.

## Shared fields and outcome rules

These fields are part of every entry. An entry changes a shared field only when it says so.

- The caster must have embraced, learned the weave and met every listed prerequisite.
- Unlisted Flows have no additional requirement. All characters already start with each Flow at 1.
- Casting costs one action and Strain equal to Load. An entry can require longer casting time.
- Validate existing committed Load, new Load and every possible extra cost before commitment.
- A sustained weave uses one held slot and its committed Load. Reinforcement reuses both.
- A fixed duration ends automatically. Release, incapacitation and concentration use the core rules.
- Range and area use metres. Ordinary perception and a clear route to the target are required.
- No entry detects thoughts, creates knowledge, bypasses walls or alters an unseen body part.
- Effects and bonuses do not stack with themselves. The core situational modifier remains limited to +2.
- A physical change can remain after an instant weave ends. Its later effects follow recorded ordinary conditions.
- No new reaction turn, extra attack or hidden opposing roll is created.

**Learning:** W01 and W02 are known at creation. Every other entry takes five full study days and one chapter learning opportunity. The ordinary curriculum library and an appointed tutor are independent sources. Neither source requires friendship with a particular NPC. Optional study can wait until the character has its prerequisites. Learning never requires random experimentation.

The required rank route uses only **W01–W09, W11, W12 and W20**. The other 28 entries are optional. W10 is optional; it is not hidden inside the preceding range.

**Oaths:** apply [the sourced Oaths module](lore-and-oaths.md) to a caster with an active oath binding. Do this before spending resources. A damage entry does not grant blanket permission to attack a person. A utility entry does not bypass the check when used to injure. Do not infer active oaths from a rank label alone.

### Outcome profiles

The profile code in each entry gives its complete result bands. Choose and record the permitted cost before the roll. A safe familiar use succeeds without a roll.

| Code | Difficulty and clean result | Costly result | Setback |
|---|---|---|---|
| U | 14 normally; 18 for a declared difficult condition; 22 for an exceptional condition. Apply the entry's full effect. | Apply the full effect and the declared utility cost. | No effect; apply the same declared utility cost. |
| I | Use U difficulty. Give only the defined, correct observation. | Give that observation and apply the declared utility cost. | Give no observation; apply the declared utility cost. Never manufacture false certainty. |
| A | Use the opponent's published Guard. Remove the listed Resistance. | Remove that Resistance and take the opponent's declared Harm. | Remove none and take that Harm. |
| P | Use the entry's stated difficulty and a four-point progress track. Add 2 progress. | Add 1 progress and apply the declared counter-cost. | Add no progress and apply the counter-cost. |

A **utility cost** is either 10 additional minutes or 1 additional Strain. Select one before committing. Extra Strain is unavailable when its worst-case total would exceed the limit. In conflict, replace the utility cost with the opponent's declared Harm. Then a costly cast achieves its effect and takes that Harm. A setback achieves no effect and takes the same Harm.

A **counter-cost** is the opponent's declared Harm during conflict. Outside conflict it uses the utility-cost menu. All casts still pay their normal Load in Strain. A failed attempt needs a changed method or situation before another attempt. A progress track's changing state can support its next declared action.

### Long casts and exact time

An entry stating a minute-long or hour-long cast uses the long-cast procedure in Mechanics. Pay Strain at the start. Reserve its Load and one held slot until completion, including for an eventual instantaneous effect. The reservation is not a second copy of the weave.

Long casts cannot start during active conflict. Establish a safe interval without an immediate opposing threat first. If conflict begins, interrupt the cast before its first exchange.

One minute is 60 seconds; one hour is 3,600 seconds. The stated casting duration includes its opening action. Advance only to the next interruption or decision. No effect occurs before successful completion.

Moving, another action, lost required contact or range, release, or incapacitation interrupts the cast. Harm uses the ordinary concentration procedure, with the pending cast treated as held. Interruption frees the reservation but refunds no Strain or time. Restarting costs a full fresh cast and duration.

A successful long cast resolves its effect after the required uninterrupted time. An instantaneous effect then releases the reservation. A sustained effect keeps it without another charge. The single frozen result governs completion; interruption cannot produce a partial success.

If its declared utility cost is ten extra minutes, those 600 seconds are follow-up work after resolution. They cannot count as rest or training. An interruption retains the remaining obligation, which must finish before another cast. The effect is not silently delayed or removed during this follow-up.

W39's recovery day begins only after its one-hour cast succeeds. An interrupted cast must restart. An interrupted recovery day retains its completed recovery time. These are distinct records.

Declare foreseeable fire, collapse, injury or other irreversible danger before casting. A harmless worked example does not authorise a different dangerous use. Reject uses outside an entry's limits before any cost.

## Foundation and the six core entries

### W01 — Spark

**Requirements:** Control 1; Fire 1. **Load:** 1. **Range:** 10 m. **Target:** one small exposed fuel source or one opponent.

**Duration:** instant. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U for ignition; A for an attack.

Ignite dry tinder no larger than a hand. An attack removes 1 Resistance. It cannot automatically ignite a person, wet fuel or an entire building. A resulting ordinary fire needs fuel and air. Record any relevant spread condition.

**Example:** lighting prepared hearth tinder raises Strain from 0 to 1. Safe household use needs no roll.

### W02 — Air Hand

**Requirements:** Control 1; Air 1. **Load:** 1. **Range:** 10 m. **Target:** one visible object weighing at most 5 kg.

**Duration:** sustained. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U; A for an improvised attack.

Move the object up to 2 m per action. Manipulate an accessible simple latch. An improvised attack removes 1 Resistance. It cannot pick an unknown lock, restrain a resisting person or operate an unseen mechanism. The caster cannot ride it.

**Example:** retrieve a 2 kg satchel from a shelf six metres away. Bringing it to hand takes three actions after casting.

### W03 — Air Binding

**Requirements:** Control 2; Air 2, Spirit 1. **Load:** 2. **Range:** 10 m. **Target:** one ordinary person.

**Duration:** sustained. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U against a willing training partner; A against an opponent.

Each successful application removes 2 Resistance. At zero Resistance, the target is restrained while the Binding remains. Reinforcement takes one action and another 2 Strain. It reuses the held slot and committed Load. Track its contribution separately. Ending it restores that contribution under the core Binding rule.

The weave does not suffocate, crush, compel obedience or create lethal damage. Declare an unusually large or powerful target ineligible before committing.

**Example:** three clean applications restrain a Resistance 6 opponent. They spend 6 Strain and commit only 2 Load.

### W04 — Warning Ward

**Requirements:** Control 2; Spirit 2, Air 1. **Load:** 2. **Range:** place within 10 m. **Target:** one area up to 10 m across.

**Duration:** sustained. **Tying:** yes, using the core procedure. **Profile:** U.

Warn the caster when a physical creature crosses the boundary. Give no identity, motive or thoughts. If the warning permits a response, give +2 to one relevant surprise or escape test. It does not automatically stop an attack. It cannot distinguish friends from enemies.

**Example:** ward a camp entrance. A crossing warns the awake caster; a tied ward does not force an instantaneous waking reaction.

### W05 — Elemental Guard

**Requirements:** Control 2; Air 2, Earth 1, Fire 1, Water 1. **Load:** 2. **Range:** 5 m. **Target:** self or one person.

**Duration:** sustained. **Tying:** yes. **Profile:** U.

Reduce harm from projectiles, falling debris or ordinary flame by 1 per exchange. It can reduce harm to zero. It does not reduce melee harm or every channeling attack. It does not stack with protective equipment against the same harm.

**Example:** a falling fragment would cause Harm 2. The Guard reduces it to 1; concentration then follows the core rule.

### W06 — Healing

**Requirements:** Control 3; Water 2, Spirit 2, Air 1. **Load:** 3. **Range:** touch. **Target:** one other consenting person, or an incapacitated person needing aid.

**Duration:** instant. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Restore 4 Vitality, up to the patient's maximum. A patient can receive this effect once between full rests. It cannot heal the caster, restore Strain, remove Wounds or revive the dead. It does not erase poison, infection or another separately defined condition.

**Example:** a patient at 3 of 8 Vitality rises to 7. The caster spends 3 Strain. The patient's existing Wound remains.

### W07 — Lamp

**Requirements:** Control 1; Air 1, Fire 1. **Load:** 1. **Range:** 5 m. **Target:** one fixed point or carried object.

**Duration:** sustained. **Tying:** yes. **Profile:** U.

Provide ordinary reading light within a 5 m radius. The light has no damaging heat. It cannot blind, reveal invisible things or convey a secret message. One person can carry the marked object while it remains within range.

**Example:** one Lamp lights a table for four readers. It removes ordinary darkness there, but grants no general Scholarship bonus.

### W08 — Warming

**Requirements:** Control 1; Fire 1. **Load:** 1. **Range:** touch. **Target:** up to 10 litres of water or one 1 kg nonliving object.

**Duration:** one-minute cast; instant effect. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Raise the target's temperature by up to 20°C, with a final maximum of 50°C. Afterward it cools normally. It cannot target living tissue, worn equipment or fuel already burning. Repeated casts cannot exceed the same temperature limit.

**Example:** warm a 10-litre basin from 20°C to 40°C in one minute. The weave supplies no soap or sanitation guarantee.

### W09 — Cooling

**Requirements:** Control 1; Water 1, Fire 1. **Load:** 1. **Range:** touch. **Target:** up to 10 litres of water or one 1 kg nonliving object.

**Duration:** one-minute cast; instant effect. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Lower the target's temperature by up to 20°C, with a final minimum of 5°C. It warms normally afterward. It cannot freeze, cure fever, stop blood flow or preserve food indefinitely. It cannot target worn equipment or living tissue.

**Example:** cool drinking water from 35°C to 15°C. This changes comfort, not whether the water is safe.

### W10 — Drying

**Requirements:** Control 1; Air 1, Water 1. **Load:** 1. **Range:** 2 m. **Target:** up to 2 kg of unattended fabric.

**Duration:** ten-minute cast; instant effect. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Remove up to one litre of ordinary water from the fabric. Collect it in an existing container or let it fall away. It cannot dry a living body, remove poison or restore damaged writing. It does not produce clean drinking water from dirty fabric.

**Example:** dry a rain-soaked cloak during a stop. The ten-minute cast and any later recovery break are separate time costs.

### W11 — Draw Water

**Requirements:** Control 1; Air 1, Water 1. **Load:** 1. **Range:** 10 m. **Target:** an existing visible water source and one container.

**Duration:** sustained, maximum ten minutes. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Transfer up to 20 litres, at 2 litres per minute. The route must be open and rise no more than 5 m. It cannot create water, drain a creature, breach a sealed vessel or purify anything.

**Example:** fill a 10-litre bucket from an open cistern in five minutes. The container and water must already exist.

### W12 — Settling Water

**Requirements:** Control 1; Earth 1, Water 1. **Load:** 1. **Range:** touch. **Target:** up to 20 litres in an open vessel.

**Duration:** ten-minute cast; instant effect. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Separate visible sand, silt and grit into a sediment layer. Pour off the water afterward. It does not remove salt, dissolved poison, microbes or an unknown disease. Clear appearance does not establish safety. It does not replace the food component of Supply.

**Example:** settle muddy wash water before ordinary filtering. The process preserves the grit as evidence rather than deleting it.

## Observation, travel and rescue

### W13 — Air Sieve

**Requirements:** Control 2; Air 2, Earth 1. **Load:** 2. **Range:** 5 m. **Target:** one stationary sphere up to 3 m across.

**Duration:** sustained, maximum ten minutes. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Divert ordinary dust, ash and visible smoke particles from the sphere. Give +2 to one relevant sight or breathing-related action inside it. It does not supply oxygen, remove invisible gases or make a burning building safe. The entry creates no immunity to a continuing hazard.

**Example:** inspect a dusty collapsed shelf with +2 Notice. Structural danger still needs its own declared response.

### W14 — Sail Breeze

**Requirements:** Control 2; Air 2. **Load:** 2. **Range:** 10 m. **Target:** one small existing sail, serving a boat with at most four occupants.

**Duration:** sustained, maximum ten minutes. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Move the boat up to 50 m through still water over the duration. Steering remains an ordinary task. It cannot oppose a strong current, move a large ship or alter regional weather. It grants no automatic travel-day multiplier.

**Example:** cross a 40 m calm inlet using a borrowed dinghy. Mooring and disembarking remain separate actions.

### W15 — Quiet Pocket

**Requirements:** Control 2; Air 2, Spirit 1. **Load:** 2. **Range:** self. **Target:** a moving area up to 2 m across.

**Duration:** sustained, maximum ten minutes. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Dampen ordinary speech and footfall inside the area. Give +2 to one Stealth test where sound matters. Sounds inside also become harder for companions to hear. It cannot conceal a shout, impact, visible person or scent. It does not prevent another person's speech.

**Example:** cross a creaking floor with one quiet companion. A guard looking directly at the pair still sees them.

### W16 — Carry Voice

**Requirements:** Control 1; Air 1, Spirit 1. **Load:** 1. **Range:** 60 m. **Target:** one speaker and one visible receiving point.

**Duration:** sustained, maximum one minute. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Make ordinary speech audible at the receiving point. Anyone within 3 m there can hear it. It does not translate, authenticate, compel or conceal the words. The speaker must speak the message aloud. The path cannot pass through a closed wall.

**Example:** call instructions to a rescuer across a ravine. The message does not prove the speaker's identity to an unfamiliar listener.

### W17 — Close Lens

**Requirements:** Control 2; Air 1, Fire 1. **Load:** 1. **Range:** 1 m. **Target:** one visible nonliving surface.

**Duration:** sustained, maximum ten minutes. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Magnify a hand-sized area up to four times. Give +2 to one Notice or Scholarship test involving visible detail. It does not read erased text, identify a substance or reveal an object's past. Adequate light remains necessary.

**Example:** compare two visible seal impressions. Magnification can expose a difference; it cannot identify the unknown forger.

### W18 — Far Lens

**Requirements:** Control 2; Air 2, Fire 1. **Load:** 2. **Range:** 500 m. **Target:** one unobstructed viewing direction.

**Duration:** sustained, maximum ten minutes. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Give +2 to one Notice test for an object at least 1 m across in good light. It cannot look through walls, cancel fog or resolve writing at extreme range. Narrow focus also leaves the caster reliant on ordinary awareness nearby.

**Example:** inspect a distant bridge for a visible wagon. Its passengers' thoughts and hidden cargo remain unknown.

### W19 — Air Pocket

**Requirements:** Control 3; Air 2, Water 2. **Load:** 3. **Range:** touch. **Target:** one willing person and an existing pocket of ordinary air.

**Duration:** sustained, maximum one minute. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Keep that air around the person's mouth and nose in water up to 2 m deep. It supports one minute of breathing. It cannot create fresh air, protect against pressure or support a deeper dive. Refilling requires reaching open air and casting again.

**Example:** help a swimmer inspect a shallow submerged snag. The swimmer still needs an ordinary way to return to the surface.

### W20 — Catch

**Requirements:** Control 2; Air 2, Water 1. **Load:** 2. **Range:** 5 m. **Target:** one willing person or unresisting object up to 120 kg.

**Duration:** sustained until used, maximum one minute. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Prepare a cushion before a fall of at most 5 m. Reduce that fall's Harm by 3, to a minimum of zero. The weave then ends. It is not a reaction cast and cannot catch someone already beyond range. It cannot enable flight.

**Example:** prepare Catch before a worker lowers herself from a four-metre ledge. A declared Harm 3 fall becomes harmless if the weave remains active.

### W21 — Short Bridge

**Requirements:** Control 2; Air 2, Earth 1. **Load:** 2. **Range:** 5 m. **Target:** two stable supports with a gap at most 3 m wide.

**Duration:** sustained, maximum ten minutes. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Make one level crossing, 1 m wide, between the supports. It holds one person and gear totalling at most 120 kg. Crossing uses an ordinary action. It cannot join to another magical bridge, move its anchors or form a floating staircase.

**Example:** cross a broken stairwell one person at a time. If concentration ends, anyone still crossing faces the previously declared fall risk.

### W22 — Lifting Platform

**Requirements:** Control 3; Air 2, Earth 2. **Load:** 3. **Range:** 10 m. **Target:** one 2 m square platform space and load up to 150 kg.

**Duration:** sustained, maximum one minute. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Lift the load vertically at 1 m per action, to a maximum height of 3 m. It cannot move horizontally or lift an unwilling person. The caster cannot ride it. When lowering normally, use the same speed. Declare a drop hazard before use.

**Example:** raise a damaged beam two metres while workers secure ordinary supports. The weave is not a permanent structural repair.

### W23 — Door Brace

**Requirements:** Control 2; Air 2, Earth 1. **Load:** 2. **Range:** 5 m. **Target:** one existing door or shutter up to 2 m across.

**Duration:** sustained, maximum ten minutes. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Give the closure a four-point barrier against forced entry. Absorb up to 4 Harm directed through it, then end. Apply only Harm already declared for the exchange. It creates no extra opposing attack. It cannot repair a missing door or bar a magical passage.

**Example:** a door receives Harm 3 and then Harm 2 from two declared exchanges. The brace absorbs four total; the final point reaches the closure.

### W24 — Weather Screen

**Requirements:** Control 2; Air 2, Water 2. **Load:** 2. **Range:** 5 m. **Target:** one stationary area up to 3 m across.

**Duration:** sustained, maximum one hour. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Divert ordinary rain and wind from the area. Give +2 to one relevant camp-making or delicate outdoor task. It does not stop hail, floodwater, missiles or severe storm conditions. Sleeping requires another shelter because full rest ends held weaves.

**Example:** keep a small map table dry while deciding a route. The screen does not change that day's published travel rate.

### W25 — Gentle Unknotting

**Requirements:** Control 2; Air 2, Spirit 1. **Load:** 1. **Range:** touch. **Target:** one visible ordinary knot in rope, thread or cord.

**Duration:** one-minute cast; instant effect. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Loosen the knot without cutting the material. The caster must see its structure and touch the cord safely. It cannot unlock metal, undo a weave or remove a hidden fastening. A knot holding weight needs that weight supported first.

**Example:** preserve a tied document packet while opening its ordinary string knot. A wax seal remains intact or broken according to the actual opening method.

### W26 — Loosen Earth

**Requirements:** Control 2; Earth 2. **Load:** 2. **Range:** touch. **Target:** up to 0.25 cubic metres of exposed soil.

**Duration:** ten-minute cast; instant effect. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Loosen compacted soil for removal with ordinary tools. It does not excavate automatically, affect stone or tunnel below occupied ground. Stable supports must already exist when collapse is possible. It cannot target living tissue or a buried object directly.

**Example:** loosen soil around a lodged wheel. Workers still remove it and lift the cart by an ordinary method.

### W27 — Stone Probe

**Requirements:** Control 2; Earth 2, Spirit 1. **Load:** 2. **Range:** touch. **Target:** one nonliving stone surface, inspecting at most 1 m inward.

**Duration:** one-minute cast; instant observation. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** I.

Reveal whether an open crack or void at least 10 cm across exists within the inspected metre. Locate it to the nearest 25 cm. It cannot identify occupants, treasure, metal type or anything beyond the void. It gives no history or structural safety guarantee.

**Example:** find a hollow behind a damaged slab before lifting it. Whether it contains a drain or a chamber remains unknown.

### W28 — Mend Stone

**Requirements:** Control 3; Earth 2, Fire 1. **Load:** 3. **Range:** touch. **Target:** one clean fracture in a nonliving stone object weighing at most 10 kg.

**Duration:** one-hour cast; instant effect. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Join two matching pieces already held in alignment. Restore ordinary handling strength to that seam. It cannot replace missing material, restore a load-bearing building or erase marks. It cannot mend metal, flesh or a special artefact.

**Example:** repair a cracked stone basin using its two surviving pieces. The lost chip and recorded fracture remain evidence.

### W29 — Forge Heat

**Requirements:** Control 3; Earth 2, Fire 2. **Load:** 3. **Range:** 2 m. **Target:** up to 1 kg of bare, unattended ordinary metal.

**Duration:** sustained, maximum one hour. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Hold sufficient heat for ordinary smithing of that metal. Actual shaping requires tools and an established craft method. It cannot target worn metal, manufacture a finished object or alter a special artefact. Hot material remains hazardous after release and cools normally.

**Example:** heat a small hinge blank while a smith works it. Tools, fuel alternatives and the smith's time remain real resources.

### W30 — Water Brake

**Requirements:** Control 3; Air 2, Water 2. **Load:** 3. **Range:** 10 m. **Target:** one 5 m square patch of shallow, moderate current.

**Duration:** sustained, maximum one minute. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Reduce local surface turbulence enough to give +2 to one crossing or boat-handling test. It cannot dam a river, reverse its flow, stop a flood or immobilise a creature. The route and its existing hazards remain.

**Example:** steady a ferry's approach to a bank. A player-led crossing still uses one handling test. The ferryman can provide the declared assistance.

## Concealment, conflict and advanced care

### W31 — Steam Veil

**Requirements:** Control 2; Air 1, Fire 1, Water 1. **Load:** 2. **Range:** 10 m. **Target:** one existing litre of water and a 3 m area.

**Duration:** sustained, maximum one minute. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Create a cool visible mist using that water. Give +2 to one sight-dependent escape or Stealth test through the area. The caster is also obscured from anyone beyond it. It cannot scald, suffocate, conceal sound or selectively reveal allies.

**Example:** obscure a doorway before withdrawing. The water is spent; the weave does not automatically complete the escape.

### W32 — Air Stroke

**Requirements:** Control 2; Air 2. **Load:** 2. **Range:** 15 m. **Target:** one opponent or freestanding object up to 25 kg.

**Duration:** instant. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** A against an opponent; U against an object.

An attack removes 2 Resistance through a blunt impact. Against an object, push it at most 1 m along an open surface. It cannot cut, crush an internal organ or lift a person. It does not force a target over a lethal edge without that declared risk.

**Example:** remove 2 Resistance on a clean combat result. An oath-bound caster must first pass the applicable intended-use check.

### W33 — Flame Dart

**Requirements:** Control 2; Air 1, Fire 2. **Load:** 2. **Range:** 20 m. **Target:** one visible opponent.

**Duration:** instant. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** A.

Remove 2 Resistance with a short flame attack. It is an attack with fire, not an automatic nonlethal capture method. It cannot turn corners or bypass ordinary cover. Declare nearby fuel and spread risks before the roll. Elemental Guard applies as stated in the core rules.

**Example:** a clean permitted attack removes 2 Resistance. A costly result removes 2 and also applies the opponent's declared Harm.

### W34 — Stone Shot

**Requirements:** Control 2; Air 1, Earth 2. **Load:** 2. **Range:** 20 m. **Target:** one opponent and one existing pebble weighing at most 100 g.

**Duration:** instant. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** A.

Launch the pebble and remove 2 Resistance on a successful attack. The pebble is expended at the impact location. The shot cannot turn corners, create ammunition or bypass cover. Projectile protection applies. Declare any dangerous line of fire before casting.

**Example:** use one visible pebble against an eligible target. Keeping a pouch of stones does not grant extra attacks.

### W35 — Air Blade

**Requirements:** Control 3; Air 2, Fire 1. **Load:** 3. **Range:** 5 m. **Target:** one opponent or exposed, unworn rope up to 2 cm thick.

**Duration:** instant. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** A against an opponent; U against rope.

An attack removes 3 Resistance through a cutting effect. A utility use cuts the specified rope. It cannot cut stone, metal, hidden restraints or an internal body part. A human attack requires declared lethal intent or an explicitly adjudicated safe alternative. Apply the Oaths check when relevant.

**Example:** cut a loose mooring line after confirming that the boat is secured by another line. The severed rope remains damaged.

### W36 — Dampen Flame

**Requirements:** Control 3; Fire 2, Water 2. **Load:** 3. **Range:** 10 m. **Target:** one ordinary fire in an area up to 2 m across.

**Duration:** instant. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

Extinguish the exposed ordinary flame in that area. Hot fuel remains hot and can reignite under recorded conditions. It does not stop an explosion, remove smoke, undo burns or cancel a hostile weave. It creates no reaction cast.

**Example:** extinguish a burning table on the caster's turn. A larger room fire needs evacuation or a separately declared plan.

### W37 — Disrupt Weave

**Requirements:** Control 3; Air 2, Spirit 2. **Load:** 3. **Range:** 10 m. **Target:** one visible held weave with Load no greater than the caster's Control.

**Duration:** four-point progress task; completion is instant. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** P.

Use difficulty 14 against the holder's Control 1–2, 16 against Control 3, or 18 against Control 4. Each action spends another 3 Strain. It commits no sustained Load between actions. At four progress, end the targeted weave. The original holder can act between exchanges.

It cannot affect a weave the character cannot perceive, a tied weave, an artefact or another person's Potential. It does not prevent the target casting again. If the targeted weave ends first, this track ends without a refund.

**Example:** two clean actions disrupt a visible Load 2 barrier held by a Control 3 caster. Each check uses difficulty 16 and costs 3 Strain.

### W38 — Shield

**Requirements:** Control 3; Spirit 2. **Load:** 3. **Range:** 10 m. **Target:** one other channeler.

**Duration:** sustained. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** P, using the full Shield procedure in Mechanics.

Difficulty is 14 against Control 1–2, 16 against Control 3, or 18 against Control 4. Add 2 if the target has greater Current Capacity or already holds the Source. These conditions do not stack. This replaces the ordinary obstacle penalty. At four progress, prevent embracing and casting; end the target's held weaves.

Reinforcement reuses the slot and spends another 3 Strain. The target can physically act or use the published four-point struggle procedure. Ending the Shield restores access. It never reduces Potential or causes severing.

**Example:** a Control 3 target already holds the Source. The initial difficulty is 18. Two clean actions establish the Shield for 6 total Strain.

### W39 — Wound Treatment

**Requirements:** Control 4; Air 2, Earth 2, Fire 2, Water 2, Spirit 2; W06 known. **Load:** 4. **Range:** touch. **Target:** one other consenting patient.

**Duration:** one-hour cast followed by one patient recovery day. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** U.

After successful treatment and that recovery day, remove one Wound. This day cannot also advance another Wound's ordinary recovery. Only one pending treatment can exist for a patient. After the benefit, another W39 treatment cannot start for seven full days. Record that completion time. It restores no Vitality or Strain. It cannot target the caster, restore an organ or limb, cure severing, reverse age or revive the dead.

If the recovery day is interrupted, retain the successful treatment and resume the remaining recovery time. A setback consumes the casting cost but starts no cooldown. Use an ordinary care plan before retrying; unchanged repeated casting is not allowed.

**Example:** a patient with two Wounds completes treatment and a recovery day. One Wound remains and follows ordinary care or a later eligible treatment.

### W40 — Read a Weave

**Requirements:** Control 3; Spirit 2. **Load:** 2. **Range:** 5 m. **Target:** one visible active weave.

**Duration:** one-minute observation. **Tying:** no. **Profile:** I.

Learn its Load and whether it is held or tied. If tied, learn its remaining duration to the nearest minute. Identify its catalogue ID only if the observer already knows that entry. Otherwise report an unfamiliar pattern. It cannot reveal its caster's identity, thoughts or remote location.

Reading does not teach the weave, detect invisible residue or bypass a concealment module. Unknown patterns still need a normal source and five study days.

**Example:** inspect an unfamiliar tied warning effect. Learn Load 2 and its remaining duration, but not its creator or exact purpose.

## Availability, preservation and review

The catalogue contains forty entries, not forty mandatory lessons. The ordinary six-entry proof remains the smallest controller demonstration. Expansion can use this complete catalogue after definition validation and worked-example checks.

All forty entries have a numerical definition, outcome profile, limit and example here. That completeness does not establish lore fidelity or implementation correctness. Their explicit campaign-invention classification remains until a primary-source review supports a narrower claim.

Use no Talent requirement for these forty entries. Optional future Talent forms need separate entries and cannot become retrospective rank requirements. A newly published version cannot erase a learned weave without a stated correction and an equivalent retained benefit.
