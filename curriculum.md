# Training and the route to the shawl

This completes the proposed curriculum. It is a plan, not a running campaign. All durations, forms and assessment procedures are house rules.

Use [Mechanics](mechanics.md) for resources and [the catalogue](weave-catalogue.md) for W01–W40. The catalogue defines practical abilities. This curriculum defines learning evidence and promotion requirements.

The hundred forms below are **campaign-made exercises**, not a recovered canonical list. Their patterns, labels and order are inventions. Consult [the source register](source-register.md) and [the Oaths module](lore-and-oaths.md) for sourced setting constraints.

## 1 Required evidence

The ordinary route requires twelve practical weaves: **W01–W09, W11, W12 and W20**. W10 and the other optional entries are unnecessary. Two required weaves are known at creation. The other ten require ten learning opportunities and fifty study days.

| Review | Requirements |
|---|---|
| Accepted readiness | N01–N10 and the novice service record. Control 2, three Flows at 2, Current Capacity 5. Know W01, W02, W07, W08, W11 and W12. |
| Aes Sedai technical readiness | F001–F100 and all twelve required weaves. Control 4, every Flow at 2, Current Capacity 8. |
| Full readiness | Technical readiness, final rehearsal, and independent service and judgement records. |
| Promotion | Play the formal scenes under assessment mode. Make the player's rank, Oaths and affiliation choices explicitly. |

No specific ordinary Skill, companion, romance, rare Talent or Potential above 8 is required. A preferred teacher or Ajah cannot become the only gatekeeper.

## 2 Training and assessment

A study day uses the day's main eight-hour activity. It includes instruction, safe practice, breaks and a brief record. It cannot also count as travel, paid work or wound recovery.

After the stated days and a guided demonstration, record technical mastery without dice. Demonstrations can be summarised. The player can choose a detailed showcase without changing its cost or award.

Each record stores its stable ID, completed days, partial-day seconds, status, source, evidence event and rules version. Status is `not_started`, `in_progress`, `mastered` or `needs_bridge`. Corrections retain the original evidence.

Store all progress and elapsed time as integer seconds. One full study day requires 28,800 credited seconds. Displayed minutes never replace or round the stored value. A ten-second increment remains ten seconds.

Curriculum mastery grants no advancement points. Chapter proofs award General and Channel Points under Mechanics. Extra messages and repeated practice cannot farm either.

A review uses one or two consequential scenes. Assess an explained decision, accurate evidence and a viable response. Several methods can satisfy a proof. Never demand a secret moral answer, prescribed emotion or particular loyalty.

A review returns **ready**, **ready with follow-up**, or **needs bridge**. A follow-up cannot secretly suspend readiness. A bridge identifies one specific missing condition. Retain completed days and unrelated mastery.

### Bounded bridges

Explain the missing condition plainly. Give a guided example and an alternative method. Provide two teaching sources, normally a replacement tutor and the curriculum library.

A technical bridge takes at most five study days and one guided demonstration. Completing it establishes the technique without another random pass/fail gate. A judgement bridge uses one relevant choice or a review of an actual decision.

If confusion remains, explain the rule out of character and work through it together. Do not create an escalating chain of failed tests. Deliberate refusal requires discussion; it is not automatic abandonment of the whole route.

Free Channel Point reassignment uses the same five-day bridge when relevant. Do not charge twice for the same instruction.

## 5 Hundred-form programme

There are ten groups of ten forms. Each group takes ten study days: **one hundred days total**. Learn them during Accepted training.

These forms reuse learned effects in precise sequences. They do not grant one hundred additional adventure spells. All ordinary Load, range, consent and Strain limits apply.

Each F identifier names an executable weave pattern or sequence. Its explanation or judgement check is additional evidence, never a substitute for weaving. The ordered F001–F100 sequence belongs only to the shawl test.

Start each sequence with enough resources. Schedule recovery within the study day; never silently refill Strain between exercises. A daily summary records casts, breaks and final state without separate messages for each.

Use marked vessels, instruments, consenting assistants and inert fixtures. A fixture can display a taught pattern without producing a living-body effect. It cannot heal, diagnose or grant a new ability. Live Healing requires an existing appropriate patient.

Each form starts without committed Load unless its instructions establish a display. End its held effects and untie its tied effects before the next form. A stated expiry can end a tied effect instead. Record every cast and tying cost.

Completion means following the visible marks within one centimetre, unless the form states another tolerance. Exact geometry is campaign invention.

### Five-day final rehearsal

Use one study day for each activity:

1. Check the curriculum register, sources and required practical knowledge.
2. Demonstrate representative low-Load forms and explain their limits.
3. Demonstrate the four-weave display and orderly release.
4. Practise distractions and safe stopping without live hazards.
5. Reconcile resources and review formal-test choices and permitted costs.

No new ordinary Skill minimum appears. Retrieve missing evidence before asking for repeated work. If the evidence never existed, use a bounded bridge.

## 9 Interruptions and retained credit

Stop before an event interrupts a main activity. Retain completed study days and the partial day's integer seconds. Resume the remaining seconds later. Never credit the same interval twice.

Mastered forms do not decay merely because time passes. An absence can prompt a safe recap without a new promotion gate. Another tutor can continue the same record.

Example: a ten-day group has six days, three hours and ten seconds complete. An event interrupts it. Retain six days and 10,810 partial-day seconds. Finish the remaining 17,990 seconds before counting day seven. Then complete its last three days. A display may show hours and minutes, but storage retains the extra ten seconds.

Wound recovery uses separate main-activity days. Two Wounds with ordinary care require six recovery days. They shift the schedule without deleting proof. Required institutional teaching remains available afterward.

Food, water, travel and declared world events still matter. A month away grants no free classes and does not freeze an agreed project. Advance each activity only to its next event or reserved player decision.

## 10 Formal tests and the protected route

Readiness reviews and rehearsals can repeat through bounded bridges. Actual formal tests remain single events in the default story. Do not invent routine repeat formal attempts.

The Accepted test follows the N01–N10, novice service and Accepted readiness requirements. Use its distinct three-arch scenes. It does not require or execute F001–F100.

The shawl test follows full readiness. Execute F001–F100 in order under assessment mode, using grouped records. Each form includes its actual taught pattern or sequence. Do not substitute its accompanying explanation for the weave.

Play each formal test in three to five substantial scenes. Do not make one hundred rolls or issue one hundred prompts. Assessment mode preserves technical completion and does not charge expedition Strain for each form.
